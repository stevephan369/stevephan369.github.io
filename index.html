PROFILE

About me

I am a lecturer in geotechnical and civil engineering at Mientrung University of Civil Engineering (MUCE), Vietnam, and Head of the Sustainable Infrastructure and Natural Disaster Research Lab (SIN Lab). My academic interests include ground improvement with geosynthetics, natural disaster mitigation, AI applications in civil engineering, carbon solutions, and STEM education.

EDUCATIONPh.D. in Geotechnical EngineeringNational Taipei University of Technology, Taiwan
